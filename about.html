About MOVIXA STREAM

Cinema discovery, made beautiful.

Our Mission

With thousands of titles across dozens of services, finding something great to watch shouldn't be hard. MOVIXA STREAM brings trending movies and TV shows, genre browsing, trailers and streaming availability together in one fast, elegant experience.

What You Get

  • Trending, popular and top-rated movies and TV shows, organised by category.
  • HD trailers, cast, ratings, seasons and similar titles on every page.
  • Where-to-watch information so you can find legal streaming options.
  • A personal list and recently viewed history, kept on your device.
  • An installable app experience on phone and desktop.

Our Data

Movie and TV information is provided by TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
