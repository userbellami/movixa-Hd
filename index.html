Frequently Asked Questions

Is MOVIXA free?

Yes. Browsing, trailers and your list are completely free.

Can I watch full movies here?

MOVIXA is a discovery platform. You can watch official trailers, and each title shows where it is legally available to stream, rent or buy in your region.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is optional and only keeps your list and history on your device.

Where does the data come from?

Movie and TV information comes from TMDB. Trailers are provided through YouTube.
