Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Summary

We keep things simple: MOVIXA STREAM does not run a user database. Your account details, list and viewing history are stored in your own browser. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Account details (if you create an account): name, email and a salted, hashed password.
  • Your list and Recently Viewed history.
  • Preferences, such as your cookie-notice choice.

This data stays in your browser's local storage and is not transmitted to our servers. Clearing your browser data or using "Clear History" removes it.

3. Information Processed When You Use the Service

When you browse, your requests for movie and TV information pass through our server to TMDB so we can keep our API key private. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may process technical data such as IP address, device and browser type for security and operation of the site. We do not attach your name or email to these requests.

4. Third Parties

  • TMDB supplies movie and TV data and images.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) loads only when you press play on a trailer, and may collect data according to its own policy.
  • WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, X and streaming services that you choose to visit via links have their own policies.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use local storage to make the Service work (sessions, list, history, preferences). We do not use advertising cookies. A service worker may cache site files so the app loads faster and can be installed on your device.

6. How We Use Information

To operate the Service, personalise your experience on your device, maintain security and respond to your enquiries.

7. Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Because accounts live on your device, anyone with access to your device and browser profile could access them, so please use a trusted device.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You control your data: sign out, clear your history, or clear your browser's site data at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. As we hold no server-side profile, most of this is in your hands, but you can contact us via with any request.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
